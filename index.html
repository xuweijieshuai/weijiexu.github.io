I am a Senior Applied Scientist at Amazon working at LLM Evaluation and LLM Agent. I am currently leading a system on application-based GenAI evaluations in Amazon.

My experience in Amazon includes unifying internal prediction tasks such as Safety and Headcount Forecasting by creating a tabular deep learning model architecture, building a weakly-suprvised topic modeling system to analyze millions of internal documents, and building an agent system to replace survey for internal usecase. As a byproduct, I have published papers in top-tier conferences including ACL and EMNLP on those topics.

Before joining Amazon, I was a Senior Data Scientist at Schlumberger where I co-created the first data science group focused on solving employee attrition problems using tabular deep learning.

I received my Master of Arts in Statistics from UC Berkeley (2018) and Bachelor of Science in Applied Mathematics with a minor in Computer Science from the University of Toronto (2017).
